# DarshLabs

## Build AI. Engineer impact.

DarshLabs is an applied AI engineering learning initiative. We help software engineers, Python developers, data scientists, and technology professionals move from AI experiments to practical systems they can build, evaluate, deploy, and explain.

## What we focus on

- **Generative AI engineering:** LLM application design, structured outputs, retrieval-augmented generation, and evaluation.
- **Agentic AI engineering:** Tool calling, stateful workflows, integrations, MCP, guardrails, and human approval.
- **Applied AI systems:** Testing, reliability, security, observability, deployment, and operational trade-offs.
- **Forward deployment engineering:** Customer discovery, rapid prototyping, system integration, validation, deployment, and handoff.

## Programs

### Production-Ready GenAI and Agentic AI Engineering

An eight-week, project-led live weekend cohort covering the engineering lifecycle for practical GenAI applications and AI agents. Learners work through guided labs and build a capstone.

### Forward Deployment Engineer: Applied AI Engineering

An upcoming specialist program for engineers who want to turn customer and business problems into deployed AI solutions. The planned curriculum spans discovery, prototyping, RAG, agentic workflows, integration, evaluation, deployment, and customer handoff. Format, duration, and cohort details are in development.

## How we learn

**Discover -> Design -> Build -> Evaluate -> Deploy -> Demonstrate**

We emphasize hands-on implementation, clear problem framing, measurable evaluation, responsible engineering, and practical delivery over theory alone.

## Who we serve

Software engineers, Python developers, data scientists, ML engineers, technical leads, and builders growing their applied AI skills.
