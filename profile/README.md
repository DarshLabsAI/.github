# DarshLabs @ `www.DarshLabs.com`
  
## Build AI. Engineer impact.

## What DarshLabs Does and Who It Serves

DarshLabs is an applied AI engineering learning initiative. We help software engineers, Python developers, data scientists, ML engineers, technical leads, and technology professionals move from AI experiments to practical systems they can build, evaluate, deploy, and explain.

## Technical Focus Areas

- **Generative AI engineering:** LLM applications, structured outputs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and evaluation.
- **Agentic AI engineering:** Tool calling, stateful workflows, API integrations, MCP, guardrails, and human approval.
- **Applied AI systems:** Testing, reliability, security, observability, deployment, and operational trade-offs.
- **Forward deployment engineering:** Customer discovery, rapid prototyping, system integration, validation, deployment, and customer handoff.

## Programs and Learning Outcomes

### Production-Ready GenAI and Agentic AI Engineering

An eight-week, project-led live weekend cohort. Learn to build practical GenAI applications and AI agents, and complete a capstone project.

### Forward Deployment Engineer: Applied AI Engineering

An upcoming specialist program for engineers who want to turn customer and business problems into deployed AI solutions. The planned curriculum spans customer discovery, prototyping, RAG, agentic workflows, integration, evaluation, deployment, and customer handoff. Duration and cohort details are in development.

## What You'll Build

Learn by developing practical AI applications, including:

- RAG applications that retrieve answers from domain documents.
- AI agents that interact with tools and APIs.
- Stateful workflows with validation and human approval.
- Evaluation pipelines that measure quality and reliability.
- Deployable AI applications with testing, monitoring, and documentation.

The goal is not just to build an impressive demo, but to understand how to evaluate, improve, and deliver an AI solution.

## Our Engineering Principles and Learning Lifecycle

- **Problem first:** Start with the business problem and user workflow.
- **Build, don't just prompt:** Develop working applications and integrations.
- **Evaluate with evidence:** Measure outcomes and investigate failures.
- **Design for reliability:** Account for security, cost, latency, and errors.
- **Deliver end to end:** Deploy, document, demonstrate, and improve.

**Discover -> Design -> Build -> Evaluate -> Deploy -> Demonstrate**
